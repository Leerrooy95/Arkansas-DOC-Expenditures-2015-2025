### 6. Independent Media Corroboration

The Arkansas Advocate began covering Arkansas prison costs in December 2025, approximately one month after this repository was created (November 2025). Key articles:

- **"Overcrowded: Arkansas' prison system costs inmates, taxpayers"** — Arkansas Advocate, December 17, 2025
  - Confirms prison population of 18,997 (including county jail backup) as of December 9, 2025
  - Covers financial burden on both inmates and taxpayers
  - URL: https://arkansasadvocate.com/2025/12/17/overcrowded-arkansas-prison-system-costs-inmates-taxpayers/

- **"Overcrowded: Protect Arkansas Act to drive continued prison population growth for at least a decade"** — Arkansas Advocate, December 19, 2025
  - Projects prison population growth of ~3,000 over the next decade due to mandatory minimums
  - URL: https://arkansasadvocate.com/2025/12/19/overcrowded-protect-arkansas-act-to-drive-continued-prison-population-growth-for-at-least-a-decade/

**Significance**: The Arkansas Advocate's independent reporting arrived at figures consistent with this repository's analysis, providing external validation of the expenditure data compiled here.
